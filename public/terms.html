Appointments and cancellations

Clients book without an account by selecting an available time and providing their first name, last name, and phone number. The booking page displays the haircut price and duration. Appointment times use the studio time zone shown during booking.

Your appointment is confirmed when the site shows a confirmation. An online payment attempt or pending checkout alone does not confirm an appointment. Save the private management link shown after confirmation; anyone with that link can view or cancel the appointment.

You can cancel through that link before the appointment starts. For other changes or assistance, contact L2 Cuts. Changes to availability do not automatically cancel an existing appointment.

Prices and payments

Prices are shown in US dollars. When a price range is offered, choose a haircut amount within that range before confirming. That amount is your booking total. You may choose payment in person or online payment when it is available. Online payments are handled by Stripe. Existing appointments retain the price and duration shown when they were booked.

Cancelling an appointment does not automatically issue an online-payment refund. Contact L2 Cuts at coleslad31@gmail.com about a refund or payment issue.

Consent: the recipient must agree to receive these alerts before an authorized admin saves the recipient's number and enables text notifications in Admin → Notifications.
