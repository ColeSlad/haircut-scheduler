Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

About this policy

L2 Cuts operates the haircut booking website at www.l2cuts.com. This policy explains how L2 Cuts handles information when you book an appointment, submit a review, browse the site, or receive authorized SMS alerts.

Information we collect

  • Bookings: first and last name, phone number, appointment date and time, service, price, payment choice and status, and booking or cancellation records. Clients do not need an account.
  • Payments: when online payment is available and you choose it, Stripe handles payment details and may collect your email for a receipt. L2 Cuts stores payment references and status; the booking app does not store full card numbers.
  • Reviews and media: the name, rating, and text you submit as a review, and photos or videos provided for publication. Published names, reviews, and gallery media are visible to site visitors.
  • Admin and SMS settings: admin email addresses, protected password records, sign-in sessions, the designated notification phone number, notification preferences, and SMS content and delivery records.
  • Technical information: hosting and security logs may include IP addresses, request times, and browser or device information. We use necessary cookies and browser storage for sign-in, checkout returns, and preventing duplicate submissions.

How we use information

We use information to schedule and manage haircuts, prevent overlapping or duplicate bookings, process payments when selected, publish reviews and authorized media, answer support requests, secure admin access, and protect the site from abuse.

When booking texts are enabled, appointment details, including client contact information, may be sent to the designated barber so the barber can manage appointments. The current SMS program sends internal booking and cancellation alerts to the consenting barber or authorized studio recipient. Entering a phone number while booking does not enroll a client in SMS messages.

SMS privacy and marketing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS phone numbers, notification preferences, and consent information are used to provide and manage the messaging program. We do not use the barber notification program to send promotional messages. Recipients can reply STOP to the sending number to opt out or contact L2 Cuts for help.

Service providers and disclosures

We use service providers to operate the site: Vercel for hosting and public media storage, Neon for database hosting, Twilio for SMS delivery, and Stripe for online payments when enabled. We share the information needed to provide those services, such as message content with the SMS provider or payment details with Stripe. This operational sharing is not sharing for third-party marketing.

TikTok embeds and links may send technical information to TikTok when you load or use them. Third-party services handle information under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably needed to address fraud, security, or legal claims.

Retention, security, and your choices

We retain information as needed to manage appointments, payments, support, security, and applicable recordkeeping obligations. We use access controls and protected admin credentials to restrict access to private appointment information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your private appointment management link confidential.

You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, including a published review or image. We may need to verify your identity and retain some records for payment, security, or legal reasons.
